MorphMAS: head-from-preset, keep body on target

What it does

  MorphMAS merges ACTIVE non-default morphs on the PERSON you attach it to
  and can export HEAD-only vs BODY vs whole morphs (.vmi / .vmb). It fixes
  head height (optional preview) and can shut off auto-behaviours and pose
  morphs at export time.

What it does NOT do

  Morph files are vertex deltas and bone formulas, not hair meshes. Hair
  from the preset is NOT baked into that morph—copy hair on the target
  (appearance preset, hair selectors, Save Hair, etc.) after you dial the new
  head morph.

Typical workflow (e.g. Morrigan preset / any Saves/Person JSON)

  1) Spawn a temporary PERSON and load your donor preset until the HEAD
     shape (and optionally hair preview) matches what you want to bake.

  2) Add MorphMAS to that same atom. Leave "Disable auto behaviours" /
     "Disable pose morphs" ON unless you rely on pose morphs for the bake.

  3) Next: turn ON only morphs that should bake into ONE merged slider.
     (Only active, non-default morphs are listed; reset-to-default sliders
     will not appear—raise them before export.)

  4) Next: enable "Splitted head/body morphs", enable "Export head morph",
     turn OFF body / whole exports if you truly want HEAD ONLY. Toggle
     "Auto fix head height" if previews need it.

  5) Export to the VaM picker path (often under Custom/Atom/Person/Morphs).
     HARD RESET or restart VaM if the UI says the new morph won't show yet.

  6) TARGET person (keep their body sliders): reload scene or refresh
     morphs, dial your exported merged HEAD morph toward 1, then APPLY the
     donor HAIR styling separately (preset hair / appearances).
